About

I don't just build Shopify stores — I engineer storefronts that move revenue. Faster load times, cleaner UX, and checkout flows that turn browsers into buyers.

I'm John Paul — a Shopify Growth Specialist. Over the past 3+ years I've partnered with eCommerce brands and agencies across the United States and Singapore, shipping custom Shopify themes and conversion systems that hold up at scale.

FAQ

The questions serious buyers ask.

Straight answers before you spend a dollar or a minute on a call.

Will you touch my live store while it's making sales?
No. I work on a duplicate theme or a staging environment and keep everything in Git. Your live store only changes when you review and approve it — and if anything looks off, we roll back in minutes.
How do we communicate across timezones?
I work daily with US and Singapore brands, so overlap is built into my schedule. You get written progress updates, a shared task board, and scheduled calls in your working hours — you're never waiting a full day for a reply.
Do you work white-label with agencies?
Yes — a good share of my work is white-label for agencies. I can operate under your brand, join your tools and standups, and communicate with your client directly or stay fully behind the scenes. NDAs and subcontractor terms are no problem.
How is pricing structured — hourly or fixed?
Fixed, scope-based pricing for projects — you approve the cost before I start, with no surprise invoices. For ongoing work, I offer monthly retainers. I intentionally don't bill open-ended hourly, because it rewards slow work; my incentive should be your results.
What if I already have a theme — can you just improve it?
Absolutely. Plenty of clients don't need a rebuild — they need their existing store to load faster and convert better. That's exactly what the Store Audit is for: I find the highest-leverage fixes and we ship them without starting over.
