How I run a store

A weekly loop.
No guesswork.

Every week follows the same loop: find the leak, test the fix, keep what wins, and tell you exactly what moved.

01

Onboard & Audit

Week one: a full read of your analytics, theme, apps, and funnel, turned into a CRO and SEO roadmap ordered by revenue impact.

02

Find the Leaks

Every week I review the numbers, session recordings, and reviews to spot where shoppers drop off and why.

03

Hypothesise & Test

Each fix starts as a clear hypothesis and ships as an A/B test on product pages, offers, or cart, built by me with no dev queue.

04

Ship What Wins

Winners go live sitewide, built on a duplicate theme first so the live store is never at risk. Losers get documented, not repeated.

05

Launch & Promote

Offers, bundles, and seasonal campaigns planned ahead and launched on time, from fall sales to Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

06

Report What Moved

A short weekly report covering tests run, results, what shipped, and what's next. You always know where your store stands.
